# Industry-Sponsored Project Brief

**Client:** William Tikiob — Mechanical Engineering and Construction Corporation (MEC2)

## Problem Overview

MEC2 currently has **limited visibility into construction materials** from delivery through installation. Materials frequently arrive on pallets with little notice and vague packing slips, which leads to:

- Misplaced material
- Reorders
- Lost field productivity

## Proposed Application

A **mobile-first inventory and material-tracking system** that:

- Logs deliveries (including photos of packing slips)
- Tracks quantities and physical locations (warehouse, yard, jobsite)
- Allows field crews to request material
- Provides visibility and accountability for project managers

## Implementation Flexibility

The client is intentionally flexible on how the solution is built. Students may choose:

- Any tech stack or architecture
- Cloud-hosted or managed services
- Third-party tools where appropriate

MEC2 is willing to cover reasonable costs for external services (e.g., cloud hosting or Amazon OCR for parsing packing slips) if it results in a better final product. **The priority is usability and real-world effectiveness**, not building everything from scratch.

## Support From MEC2

They can provide:

- Clear requirements and workflows
- Access to end users for feedback (warehouse, field, PMs)
- Realistic test scenarios and data
- A genuine deployment target beyond a demonstration
- Additional problems if the students go above and beyond
